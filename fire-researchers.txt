FIRE RESEARCHERS
================


Francesca Di Giuseppe
---------------------
ECMWF (European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts), Reading, UK
Project manager, Fire Danger Forecasting service

Fire and drought prediction. Built much of ECMWF's fire danger modelling
infrastructure and runs the operational fire forecasts for the EU's Copernicus
Emergency Management Service (CEMS). Lead author on a Nature Communications
paper introducing a machine-learning approach that forecasts fire activity
itself rather than only fire danger.

Janice L. Coen
--------------
NSF NCAR (National Center for Atmospheric Research), Boulder, CO
Project Scientist

Wildland fire behavior and its coupling to weather. Co-developer of CAWFE, the
Coupled Atmosphere-Wildland Fire Environment model, where predicted winds drive
fire growth and the fire's own heat release feeds back into the atmosphere.
Also analyses infrared imagery of wildfires and prescribed burns directly --
the closest overlap with EmberWorks' sensing work of the three.

LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/janice-coen-8269b572/
NCAR:     https://staff.ucar.edu/users/janicec

Faraz Hedayati
--------------
IBHS (Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety)
Lead Research Engineer

Vulnerability of structures to fire in the wildland-urban interface. Work on
firebrand (ember) generation from wildland and structural fuels, how embers
ignite building components such as decks, siding and windows, and wind-driven
building-to-building spread and safe separation distances. Interests include
heat transfer models feeding physics-based risk modelling, post-fire field
investigation, and machine learning and vision for community wildfire risk.
PhD in Mechanical Engineering, UNC Charlotte.

LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/farazhedayati/
IBHS:     https://ibhs.org/team/faraz-hedayati/

Not an author on anything currently in this library.
